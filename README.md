Executive OverviewJNova Pulse is an enterprise-grade, mobile-responsive marketing automation and campaign management platform engineered specifically for secure, hyper-targeted outreach across WhatsApp, SMS, and major social media networks (Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, TikTok).Designed to operate with a robust base currency in Nigerian Naira (NGN) and international gateways, JNova Pulse enables businesses, political organizations, and agencies to broadcast rich media campaigns down to specific administrative regions—such as State $\rightarrow$ Local Government Area (LGA) $\rightarrow$ Ward—while strictly protecting consumer privacy through advanced backend data masking and role-based access control.🚀 Key Features & ModulesSecure Onboarding & 3-Day Free Trial (UC-01 to UC-03): New businesses register securely with password policy enforcement and receive an automatic 3-day free trial for WhatsApp advertising tools.Hierarchical Audience Segmentation (SEC-010 / SEC-011): Ingest and classify contact databases by Nigerian States, LGAs, and Wards without exposing raw Personally Identifiable Information (PII) to standard users.Multi-Channel Campaign Builder: Compose and schedule image and video ads for WhatsApp Business API, broadcast bulk SMS credits, and publish content across connected social media accounts.Multi-Currency Payment Gateway & FX (UC-PAY-016 / UC-PAY-017): Tiered subscription packages (Starter, Growth, Business, Agency) with real-time foreign currency conversion (USD, GBP, EUR) to Nigerian Naira (NGN) and independent webhook verification.Autonomous Self-Healing Reliability Engine: Continuous operational monitoring of core user journeys (Login $\rightarrow$ Audience Selection $\rightarrow$ Campaign Creation $\rightarrow$ Scheduling $\rightarrow$ Dispatch $\rightarrow$ Delivery $\rightarrow$ Payment $\rightarrow$ Subscription $\rightarrow$ Reporting) with automated fault detection, exponential backoff retries, and incident logging.Mobile-Responsive UI: Fluid layouts, sliding drawers, and touch-optimized controls ensuring seamless usage across mobile phones, tablets, and desktops.🛠️ Project Scope & Architecture[1. Onboarding & Auth] $\rightarrow$ [2. Audience Targeting] $\rightarrow$ [3. Campaign Composition] $\rightarrow$ [4. Multi-Channel Dispatch] $\rightarrow$ [5. Analytics & Audit]
User Lifecycle & Security: Multi-factor authentication policies, role-based access control (Admin, Business Owner, Marketer, Finance, Viewer), audit logging, and automated self-healing reliability monitoring.Audience Database & Segmentation: Secure ingestion, validation, and hierarchical classification of contact databases by Nigerian States, LGAs, and Wards without exposing raw phone numbers.Multi-Channel Campaign Builder: Creation, scheduling, media uploading (images and videos), caption composing, and instant dispatch for WhatsApp Business API, Bulk SMS gateways, and connected social media accounts.Financial & Subscription Management: Tiered subscription packages with multi-currency payment gateway integration and real-time FX conversion to NGN.Autonomous Reliability & Analytics: Real-time operational monitoring, automated fault detection and exponential backoff retries across core user journeys, delivery tracking, geographic performance reporting, and exportable audit logs.🎯 Project ObjectivesHyper-Local Precision Targeting: Enable campaigns to reach targeted audiences at the grassroots level (Ward and LGA levels) while maintaining 100% compliance with data privacy standards.Multi-Channel Operational Efficiency: Consolidate WhatsApp, SMS, and cross-platform social media publishing into a single unified dashboard, reducing campaign setup time by over 60%.Strict Data Privacy & Security: Prevent unauthorized viewing or export of raw subscriber phone numbers through robust backend isolation and cryptographic masking.Resilient Enterprise Uptime: Maintain continuous operational availability via an autonomous self-healing agent that detects API, database, or payment anomalies and executes safe, controlled recovery actions.Seamless Financial
